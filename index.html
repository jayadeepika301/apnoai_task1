01 — ABOUT THE PROJECT

Fitness shouldn't feel complicated.

FitAI is a college project idea designed to make fitness more approachable and personalized. Instead of switching between different apps, users can find workout guidance, nutrition suggestions and progress tracking in one place.

The bigger idea is to use AI and computer vision responsibly to help users understand exercise form and stay consistent with their goals.
